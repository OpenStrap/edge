the OpenStrap name or logo.

## Forks

You are free to fork, modify and redistribute the code under the AGPL. If you
distribute a modified version (an app, a package, a hosted service), you must:

- give it a different name that does not contain "OpenStrap" and could not be
  confused with it;
- replace the OpenStrap logo, app icon and other branding with your own;
- not suggest that your version is official, endorsed by or supported by OpenStrap.

Saying your project is "based on OpenStrap" with a link to
https://github.com/OpenStrap is fine, and appreciated.

## What is always fine

- Using the name to refer to the project, for example in an article, a review, a
  comparison or a bug report.
- Unmodified builds published by the OpenStrap project itself.
- Saying your device or service works with OpenStrap, if it does.

Questions about a specific use: open an issue in https://github.com/OpenStrap/edge.
