A health app for your wearables.

No subscription, no fees, private by default. Edge pairs with your device over Bluetooth and works out your sleep, recovery, strain and the rest entirely on your phone. No account, and no server that ever receives your health data.

It runs on your phone

The band talks to the app, the app decodes the bytes and computes every metric locally, and the results are stored on the device. There’s no server that sees your health data — that isn’t a setting, it’s the architecture.

Precisely, because “no cloud” gets said too loosely: GitHub Release builds send anonymous crash diagnostics by default (switchable off); store builds send none. Neither ever includes health data. Detail →

Published methods, cited

Banister TRIMP, Cole–Kripke, Lomb–Scargle, van Hees and friends. Nothing invented, no neural net guessing at anyone’s formulas. You can go read the paper and decide whether you trust the number.

It admits what it doesn’t know

Every metric carries a confidence and a tier. When the data isn’t there you get a dash, not a plausible-looking guess. SpO2 and skin temperature are relative-only, because that’s what the band actually gives up.

Its own numbers

Different algorithms from any device maker’s app, so expect different numbers. Textbook methods over the data your device records. It trends correctly and it’ll tell you when you’re under-recovered.

Before you install

  • Close the device maker’s app before pairing. Bluetooth only lets one app own a device at a time.
  • Pick one and stay on it. A firmware update pushed from the device maker’s app could change the records this depends on, and there’s no fixing that from here.
  • Not medical-grade. These are approximations from published research, not validated against a lab. Nothing here is a diagnosis.
  • There are bugs. It’s a beta built by one person. Open an issue when something looks wrong — that’s genuinely the most useful thing you can do.

How it’s put together

band → Bluetooth → protocol decoder → local storage → analytics → the UI

edge The app. Bluetooth, storage, the compute pipeline, every screen. protocol The decoders. Raw band bytes into named fields. Pure Dart, zero dependencies. analytics The math. Published, peer-reviewed methods with citations in the code. research The lab notebook. The protocol written down, plus a Python client.
