Open Food Facts

Contains information from Open Food Facts, which is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0. Individual contents of the database are available under the Database Contents License.

When you scan a food barcode, Edge asks Open Food Facts about that barcode and shows you the nutrition figures on file, which you can then edit before saving. Edge does not fetch or display Open Food Facts product photographs: those are licensed separately, per photographer, and may carry third-party trademark restrictions of their own.

Open Food Facts' terms state that the data must not be used for medical purposes. Edge treats a scanned figure as something you entered, never as something it measured, and drops any value that fails a basic plausibility check rather than showing it to you.

wger exercise data

Edge's exercise picker contains a versioned offline snapshot of selected fields from the wger exercise database. The app does not contact wger while you browse or record a workout.

wger licenses the base exercise and each translation separately. Each record shipped by Edge retains its source UUID and every distinct author/license pair used by its base metadata, labels, or aliases. The snapshot currently accepts records under CC BY-SA 3.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, or CC0. The generated dataset remains under those licenses and is separate from Edge's MIT-licensed application code. The source/credits button beside each imported exercise opens its exact public wger record, including the license and any author information supplied by wger.

Edge adapts the source by selecting exercise names and aliases, muscle classifications, equipment, categories, and translation labels; normalizing whitespace; and sorting the result. It contains no wger images, videos, notes, or exercise descriptions.
