# Live guides

A live guide shows you where something is in Edge, on your own screens. It dims the parts of the screen you do not need, points at the real control to tap, and tells you in a short card what to do next. You do the taps yourself.

There are eight guides. Each answers one "where do I find this?" question.

## Start a guide

1. On the **Today**, **Sleep**, **Activity** or **Health** tab, tap the round profile button at the top right. Its screen reader label is "Profile and settings".
2. On the **Profile** screen, under **Quick access**, tap **Help**.
3. Tap a guide. The list is grouped under the tab each guide starts on.

The guide closes the screens above the tabs, opens the tab it starts on, and shows step 1.

The wording in these pages is English. In another language the app shows its own words, and the guides quote those.

All eight guides work with WHOOP 4 and WHOOP 5.

## What you see on a step

- **A moving hand and highlight.** The hand points at the real control. When a step offers several controls, the hand visits them and the label says "tap one of" or "search or pick". With reduced motion, a static hand and outline replace the animation.
- **Scrolling to the target.** The guide brings a hidden target into view. Touch the page to stop that movement; the guide then leaves scrolling to you for that step. If the target is still off screen, a "scroll" label points the way.
- **The card around it stays as it is.** The card the control sits in is shown normally.
- **The cards just above and just below are dimmed.** They are still there and still readable, but faded.
- **Every other card becomes a grey placeholder.** The grey bars stand in for its text and charts. This keeps your eye on the part of the screen that matters. The placeholders appear at once. The dimming fades in over under a second, and the pointing aid appears when it finishes. If your phone has animations turned off, the fade, hand movement and placeholder pulse are skipped.
- **Some things are never covered.** Icons, images, card titles, the screen title, the top bar and the tab bar are never turned into placeholders. A card title in a placeholder card stays, dimmed.

At the bottom of the screen, above the tab bar, is the step card. It shows the step number, what to do (the names of buttons and sections from the app are in bold), a row of dots that shows how far along you are, **Don't see it?** and **Exit**.

The card never covers the control you need to tap. If the control would be under the card, the card moves out of the way.

## What taps do

- **The highlighted control works normally.** Tap it and the app does what it always does. Starting a workout really starts it, and saving a nap really saves it. In the activity picker, the guide opens the first activity group once so you can see its rows. It leaves a group you have already opened alone. You choose the activity and perform the action yourself.
- **Dimmed controls work too.** If you tap one, the app opens it. If the screen it opens is not part of the guide, you have left the path (see below).
- **Grey placeholders do not take taps.** You can still scroll the page by dragging on them.
- **The card's buttons work,** and so do Android's system Back and the arrow at the top left of a page. On iPhone, the edge swipe for Back does not work on a guide's screens while the guide is running. Use the arrow at the top left of the page.
- **Pickers and dialogs.** When the app asks you something in a pop-up, such as a time picker or a confirm box, the guide steps aside and hides its card. It comes back when the pop-up closes.

## Don't see it?

Most guides add a **Don't see it?** link to the card. Tap it and the card opens a short explanation of why the control might be missing, such as a card that only shows while something is waiting. Tap it again to close it. A guide with nothing to add, like [Where to start and stop a workout](guide-start-workout.md), has no link.

## Leaving the path

If you go to a screen that is not part of the guide, the card changes to "You've left the path" with the line "This screen isn't part of the guide." Nothing is a grey placeholder in this state, only dimmed. The control that leads back stays normal and gets the pointing aid. The surrounding content is dimmed. It is either a tab or the back arrow of a page.

The card also says how many taps lead back, such as "2 taps back". Tap **Back to the guide** and the guide makes those taps for you, one screen at a time. If the guide has never matched a step yet, there is no **Back to the guide** button, and you go back with the highlighted control.

When you reach the matching screen again, the guide picks up on the right step.

## Finishing and leaving

Tap an offered control on the last step and the card changes to "Done" with "That was the last step." Some guides add notes there, such as what deleting a workout does.

Tap **Exit** at any time. The guide goes away and you stay on the screen you are on. Actions you have completed stay in effect. Guide progress is not saved.

## Move the card

The card sits at the bottom or the top of the screen. To move it, drag it by its blank space, not by a button. Your position holds for the rest of that guide. While you scroll, the card holds its position and fades if it would cover the target. After scrolling stops it moves to a clear spot. Long card text scrolls inside the available space. The next guide you start begins with the card back in its automatic place.

## With a screen reader

- When a step starts, the screen reader announces the step text. The off-path and done titles are announced too.
- The outlined control has a button named "Guide target:" followed by the step text. Activating it taps the real control.
- When you are off the path, the highlighted way back is a button named "Guide: open the Sleep tab" (with the name of the tab) or "Guide: back to the path".
- The card has two custom actions, **Move card up** and **Move card down**.
- The progress dots read as "Step 2 of 3".
- **Exit** is read as "Exit the guide". **Don't see it?** is a button that says whether it is open or closed.
- The dimmed and grey content stays reachable to a screen reader. The guide does not hide it.
