# Lexicon

## Guides

- Navigation node: a declared shell tab, sub-tab, page or sub-page, present while its content loads. Steps name their node in `guide_navigation.dart`.
- Hop: one real navigation action toward a step, using Back, a shell tab, a sub-tab or a page entry.
- Layout sibling: the previous or next unit in the target's enclosing vertical layout. Transforms and viewport clipping preserve this relation.
- Placeholder: replacement text bars, an empty ring track or a muted plot sine, painted over the existing surface. Icons, images and card titles keep their source paint.
- Unscoped guide content: a page-wide selector, such as every pressable row. It does not identify a particular control on a neighbouring step.

## Sleep staging

- Staging provenance: a night's requested method, effective method and observed seconds that retained the original labels after an HMM abstention. `hmm_cardio` means HMM and cardio both contributed; `hmm_vendor_staged` means HMM and a paired device both contributed. An absent field means unknown; it does not mean cardio or zero fallback. Avoid calling the requested method the effective method.
