Frequently asked questions

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Getting started

How do I install Edge?

On iPhone, join the TestFlight beta. On Android, download the APK from the latest release. One app works with WHOOP 4.0, 5.0 and MG.

How do I update?

On iPhone, the TestFlight app installs new builds (you get an email when one is ready). If automatic updates are off, open TestFlight and tap Update. On Android, open the newer APK and it installs over the old one, keeping your data.

Will my TestFlight access expire?

Each TestFlight build lasts 90 days. New builds are published before that, and your access carries over to them.

Android says the app was blocked by Play Protect

Play Protect warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that ask for sensitive permissions. Edge's optional feature that buzzes the band for phone notifications needs notification access, which triggers the warning. The APK on the releases page is the one built from this repository's source.

How can I check a build is the real one?

Releases are built by GitHub Actions from the tagged source and published on the releases page, which lists a checksum for each file. Only install builds from there or from the TestFlight link above.

Pairing and syncing

My band won't pair, or it says connected but no data arrives

  1. Forget the band in Edge and in your phone's Bluetooth settings.
  2. Take the band off your wrist and wait for the green light to go off.
  3. Tap the top of the band firmly until it flashes blue (pairing mode).
  4. Pair again from Edge.

If it keeps happening, send a report from Profile → Settings → Report an issue so the logs reach us.

Can I use the official WHOOP app at the same time?

Don't. The band sends its data to one app at a time, so two apps compete for it. The official app can also install firmware updates on the band; Edge is tested on the firmware your band has now, so keep the band away from it.

Does Edge need to stay open?

No. Edge keeps syncing in the background. If you force-close it, it catches up the next time it opens, which can take a few minutes after a long gap.

Scores

Why don't I have a recovery score yet?

Edge learns your personal baseline from its own measurements, which takes about 14 nights of wear. Until then, recovery stays empty instead of showing a number it can't stand behind.

I imported my WHOOP history. Why do I still have to wait 14 nights?

Your WHOOP history is imported and shows up in your history and trends. But those numbers were calculated by WHOOP's own methods, which measure HRV and resting heart rate differently. Using them as your baseline would compare every new night from Edge against a different method and skew your scores, so the baseline comes from Edge's own nights.

Your data

Can I import my WHOOP or NOOP data?

Yes, from Profile → Export and backup → Import a file. A WHOOP data export brings in your daily recovery, sleep and strain. A NOOP export brings in its recorded sensor data, which Edge then scores itself.

I'm getting a new phone. How do I keep my data?

Everything lives on your phone, so deleting the app deletes your data. Before you switch, go to Profile → Export and backup and export the database or an encrypted backup, move the file to the new phone, import it there with Import a file, then pair your band again. The backup holds your data but not your profile or settings, so enter those again on the new phone.

Does my data leave my phone?

Your health data stays on your phone unless you choose to send it, for example to Apple Health or Health Connect, an AI coach you set up, or an issue report. Two other things can leave it: anonymous crash reports, only while Settings → Crash reports is on, and barcodes you scan in the food log, which are looked up on Open Food Facts (turn that off with Settings → Look barcodes up online). Neither includes your health data. The privacy policy has the detail.
